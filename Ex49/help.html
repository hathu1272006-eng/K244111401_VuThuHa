Online Classifieds Search Help

Step 1: Select a classified section from the options (Employment, Personal, For Sale, etc.).

Step 2: Check the publications you want to include in your search.

Step 3: Enter the date range in From and To fields using the calendar display.

Step 4: Type keywords in the input box to describe what you are looking for.

Step 5: Click Search to see the results, or Cancel to clear all the values in the form.
